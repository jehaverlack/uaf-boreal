About BOREAL

Browser-based Organizer for Rclone Exploration, Audit & Lookup

BOREAL is a cross-platform desktop application for configuring, exploring, auditing, and migrating data accessible through Rclone.

BOREAL provides a local browser-based interface backed by a standalone Rust application.

BOREAL was developed by John Haverlack at the Alaska Center for Energy and Power (ACEP) at the University of Alaska Fairbanks (UAF) to facilitate Google Drive data management.
